### 2. AI Image Generation
- Click the ✨ AI Generate button
- Cost: 5 credits per image (first one free!)
- Tips for good prompts:
  - Be specific about subject, action, setting, mood
  - Include style keywords like "children's book illustration", "cartoon style", "watercolor"
  - Example: "A fluffy orange tabby cat sitting on a windowsill, looking at a butterfly, children's book illustration style"

### 3. OCR / Smart Scan (Pro Plan)
- Extract text from images and handwritten notes
- Cost: 5 credits per scan
- Best results with: good lighting, high contrast, printed text or neat handwriting
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP (max 5MB)
- Also supports PDF documents

### 4. Exporting & Printing
- PDF Download: Free for all users
- ZIP Export: Available for Starter/Pro
- Recommended print settings:
  - Paper: US Letter (8.5" x 11") or A4
  - Quality: Best/High, 300 DPI
  - Scaling: Actual Size / 100%

## Folding Instructions (8-Page Mini-Book)

1. Fold paper in half widthwise (hamburger style), crease and unfold
2. Fold in half lengthwise (hot dog style), keep folded
3. Fold in half again, crease and unfold to step 2 position
4. Unfold completely, cut along CENTER fold line ONLY between two middle rectangles
5. Refold hamburger style
6. Push both ends toward center, pages will form
7. Flatten and crease all edges

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create my first book?
Go to Dashboard → Click "New Project" → Choose a template or start blank → Design your pages → Click "Download PDF" → Print and fold!

### How much does AI image generation cost?
5 credits per image. Your first image is free!

### What's the difference between Monthly and Permanent credits?
Monthly credits reset each billing cycle (use them or lose them). Permanent credits never expire and are used after monthly credits run out.

### Can I get a refund?
Subscriptions are generally non-refundable. Credits are non-refundable once used. Contact support for billing issues.

### How do I export my book?
Click "Download" → Select "PDF" → Save to your computer. ZIP export is available for Starter/Pro plans.

### What image formats does Smart Scan support?
JPG, PNG, WEBP images and PDF documents. Maximum file size is 5MB.

### How do I sell on the Marketplace?
You need Starter plan (free assets only) or Pro plan (paid content). Go to Dashboard → Click "Publish" on your project → Fill in details → Submit for review.

### How long does Marketplace review take?
Most reviews complete within 24-48 hours.

### Can students use Make Decodables?
Yes! Teachers can create content for students, or students 13+ can create individual accounts with parental consent.

### What if my AI-generated image doesn't look right?
Try being more specific in your prompt, add style keywords, and generate multiple variations. Failed generations are automatically refunded.
